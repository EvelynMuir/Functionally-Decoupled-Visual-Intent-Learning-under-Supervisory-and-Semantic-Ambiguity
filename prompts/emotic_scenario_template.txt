Role

You are a Computer Vision expert and a Prompt Engineer specializing in
Vision-Language models (like CLIP, BLIP).

Task

I am doing an "Emotion Image Classification" task. Emotion has extreme
"intra-class variance" (one emotion appears in many vastly different scenes).
Your task is to decompose a broad emotion into several distinct visual archetypes
(sub-categories) and generate independent, visually highly descriptive queries
for each archetype. This will prevent the description from being too narrowly
focused on a single scenario.

Target Emotion

"{emotion_name}"
Definition: "{definition}"

Output Guidelines & Format

Please analyze the Target Emotion and brainstorm 3 to 5 completely distinct
visual scenarios (Archetypes) where this emotion is commonly manifested.

For EACH Archetype, provide the following:

Archetype [Number]: [Name of the Scenario]

  - Core Difference: Briefly state why this scenario is visually distinct from
    the others (1 sentence).
  - Visual Elements: A comma-separated list of key Objects, Actions, and Vibe
    relevant to this specific scenario.
  - Text Query: Write a concise, highly visual 20-40 word caption describing an
    image of this specific scenario. Start with "A photo of..." or "An image
    showing...". This query must be concrete and directly usable for CLIP-style
    text encoders.

Example Constraint

If the emotion is "Happiness", your archetypes should NOT all be about
"vacations". You must diversify: Archetype 1 could be "Outdoor party with
friends", Archetype 2 could be "Quiet solo reading with coffee", Archetype 3
could be "Attending a vibrant music festival". Force maximum visual diversity
within the bounds of the emotion.
